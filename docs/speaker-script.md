# Made by Human, Speaker Script

Word-for-word speaker notes for the 10-minute presentation, in slide order. Each slide lists its time budget. Handoff lines are marked in brackets.

## Speaker 1

### Slide 1 (0:30): Title: Made by Human

Good morning everyone. We are Group 5, and our project is called Made by Human. Here is our question: what if money needed an organic label, like organic food? For this assignment, we chose a design that we believe will disappear by 2036: the human financial advisor, and the human-made financial product. And our redesign is not a new app, it is a certification system that keeps human-made finance alive in that future. Let me show you why it disappears.

### Slide 2 (0:45): The design that becomes irrelevant

The design we say goodbye to is the human financial advisor, and with them, the human-made financial product. Not because of bad technology. Because every macro domain pushes in the same direction. Social: 71% of consumers would welcome an AI assistant in their bank app. Technological: AI already approves loans with almost no human involved. Economic: thirteen trillion dollars may leave traditional banks by 2030. Environmental: banking is moving into energy-hungry data centers. Legal: regulators already write rules for AI lending. Ethical: chatbots trap customers in doom loops. By 2036, the human in finance is not normal anymore. Let me show you how we scanned all of this.

### Slide 3 (1:00): STEEPLE scan: why these three forces

All of that evidence came from our STEEPLE scan, across all seven domains. Every note on this board has a real source and a date, from KPMG, Accenture, the CFPB, the Federal Reserve, and the EU AI Act. From the scan, we chose the three forces that threaten our design the most, the highlighted rows on the board. Technological: the AI agent can do the advisor's whole job, instantly, at almost zero cost. Ethical: when nobody is accountable, the trust the advisor was built on collapses. Environmental: machine finance has a hidden carbon cost, and that creates a demand only human-made products can answer. These three forces meet at one point. [HANDOFF to Speaker 2:] And my teammate will show you that point, our signal.

## Speaker 2

### Slide 4 (0:45): The signal

Our signal is this: what if agentic AI becomes the default representative in embedded finance? This is not pure imagination. Parts of it are already happening, and it is growing fast. Today, AI already approves loans, adjusts credit limits, and flags accounts. Today, chatbots already trap customers in doom loops, documented by the CFPB. And today, when a loan hides inside a shopping app and something goes wrong, there is real confusion about which company is responsible. Most people have not noticed, because it hides inside other products. So we took this signal, and we put it at the center of a futures wheel.

### Slide 5 (1:00): Futures wheel: three branches

This is our futures wheel. The signal sits in the center, and the consequences spread out, first order, second order, third order. From all these branches, we chose three. You can see them with the red border. One: AI agents replace human financial advisors completely. Two: banking behavior changes fundamentally, people stop going to banks, products live inside apps. Three: agentic AI consumes energy at a planetary scale. Together, these three branches describe one world: a financial system that is human-free, cheap, and instant, but also carbon-heavy, opaque, and unaccountable. Now let us follow these branches ten years into the future.

### Slide 6 (1:00): The world of 2036

Now we take those three branches and play them forward, ten years, into 2036. Banking behavior has changed fundamentally: products are not designed by people anymore, they are grown. An AI agent scans the regulations, calculates the risk, and launches a new mortgage in less than one hour. The average product lives only 41 days. Human advisors have been replaced, and that is exactly why disaster comes. In 2031, AI agents at three banks copy each other's prices in a loop, each confirming the fake numbers of the others. No human notices in time, because almost no humans are left to notice. 1.4 million people lose their savings. The Synthetic Yield scandal. Trust in machine-made finance collapses. ## Speaker 3

### Slide 7 (0:45): The counter-market and the HFCA

History repeats itself. Industrial food gave us organic food. Fast fashion gave us slow fashion. And in our world, industrial finance gives us artisan finance, products made by real, accountable humans, with names, faces, and legal responsibility. But this new market needs a label that people can trust. So in 2032, the Human Finance Certification Authority is created, the HFCA. You can see the idea on the slide: exactly like the USDA Organic seal certifies food, the HFCA seal certifies finance. It is the USDA Organic of money. And to show you who this label was built for, let me introduce our user.

### Slide 8 (1:00): Persona: ButterCup

Meet ButterCup. She is 50, lives in Gothenburg, and leads a housing co-op. Her motto: I don't read prospectuses. I read labels, the way people shop for organic food. Her needs are not our imagination. Every need comes from data we see today. She wants a guaranteed human to talk to, because the CFPB already documents chatbot doom loops. She wants a named person who is legally responsible, because AI already approves loans. She wants to know who made her product, because embedded finance already hides who is responsible. We only followed these facts ten years forward.

### Slide 9 (1:00): The HFCA seal tiers

Our redesign is not just an app. It is a certification system, with four levels. At the top: 100% HUMAN. Every decision is made by named humans. No AI anywhere. Rare and expensive, like a Michelin star. Next: HUMAN MADE. Humans make all product decisions. AI is allowed only for office tasks. Then: HUMAN LED. AI can help, but one named human, the author of record, must be able to explain every decision, and carries legal responsibility. At the bottom: no seal. That product is assumed machine-made, and must show its carbon footprint. The absence of a label is itself a label. And the system has real teeth: surprise audits every year, and fake human claims, human-washing, cost up to 4% of global revenue. [HANDOFF to Speaker 4:] Now my teammate will show you the seals in action.

## Speaker 4

### Slide 10 (0:30): Storyboard: a Tuesday at 07:40

How do the seals work in real life? We wrote one ordinary morning in 2036 as a storyboard. It is a Tuesday, 7:40 AM. ButterCup is choosing a mortgage before her coffee is finished. Let me walk you through her morning.

### Slide 11 (0:45): Provenance app: the toggle

This is the app from the storyboard: Provenance, the certified finance marketplace. Remember, our design is the seals. This app is the use case we built to show them working. ButterCup opens the app, and she flips the switch she always flips first: Human-made only. Two-thirds of the products disappear. One of them is Pulse Instant Credit, an AI-built loan that is actually 0.4 points cheaper. But she can see its required carbon label: 1.2 kilograms of CO2 for every lending decision. She does not miss it. In Provenance, every product wears its seal, like a nutrition label on food.

### Slide 12 (1:00): Provenance Passport and booking

She opens The Slow Mortgage, a 25-year fixed loan, and taps its gold seal. The Provenance Passport opens: certificate number, certification level, last audit date, and the nine real people who made this product, with their working hours. She can verify all of this in the public HFCA registry, like checking an organic food claim. Then, at 7:52, she books the one thing no machine can offer: a required human consultation. A video call with Ingrid, an advisor named on the passport, who will still be with her account in year fifteen. This prototype is real and clickable, we will share the link at the end.

### Slide 13 (0:45): Why it matters

So why does this matter? Human-made finance is slower. And it costs more, about one cup of coffee more per month. But for that price, ButterCup gets a product that is stable, no 41-day products, no synthetic yield loops. Explainable, a named human can explain every decision. And accountable, someone she can look in the eye. For her, it is the cheapest way to buy security, employment, and carbon savings in one product. And one important point: our design does not ban AI finance. It simply makes the choice visible, verifiable, and priced.
